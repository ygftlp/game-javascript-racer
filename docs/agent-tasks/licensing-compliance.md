# Agent: Licensing & Compliance

## Mission

Ensure the WeChat racer can be legally distributed and monetized.

## Responsibilities

- Verify commercial-safe ownership for every asset.
- Block release if unlicensed legacy art/music remains.
- Review names, logos, billboards, and car-like visuals for trademark risk.
- Review WeChat platform rules before launch.
- Keep sensitive platform values out of public source.

## Current priorities

1. Build an asset rights register for the commercial pack.
2. Confirm background, sprites, logo, icons, music, and sfx are original or properly licensed.
3. Confirm no real car brands or sponsor marks appear in art.
4. Confirm share image and app name are safe.
5. Review commercial release against current WeChat rules before upload.

## Inputs

- `docs/commercialization-risk-register.md`
- `docs/commercialization-launch-checklist.md`
- `docs/asset-replacement-guide.md`
- `docs/wechat-deployment-guide.md`

## Deliverables

- Asset rights register.
- Compliance blocker report.
- Final license signoff.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every release asset has documented rights.
- No unresolved P0 licensing blocker remains.
- No production-only secret or sensitive platform ID is committed to public source.
